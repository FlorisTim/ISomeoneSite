What is cool platformer?
Cool platformer is a game i made for a school project, I worked on it for a total of 2 months, the game itself is a 3D platformer in the console app, it began as a prototype before the school project. In the prototype i made a simple wireframe game in the console, with some moving platforms and y-level detection for collision.
