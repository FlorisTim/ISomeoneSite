["title"]Physics[/] ["post spckbd"] ["title"]2026 March[/] The physics is done by changing an entities y velocity by a negative value everytime it's not on ground.
when an entity hits a wall it checks if moving up and forward means that there isn't a wall, basically letting steps or stairs work.
there is one list of boxes that every entity scroll through to test if it's hitbox is overlapping with one of them, and if that is true, then the velocity applied that made that overlap happen is undone.

moving platforms are also entities, but without any physics enabled, and with a hitbox constantly set to its position.
entities with their feet on a hitbox that is moving will move with the hitbox automatically.

deltatime is calculated every frame and included in the calculations done so framerate does not affect gameplay [/]["back"]
